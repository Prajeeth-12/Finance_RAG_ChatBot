Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) is the annualized value of all term subscription licenses, support obligations (including those attributable to perpetual licenses) and Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) contracts that are active on the last day of the quarter. In respect of IP Partnerships, ARR is computed based on annualized value of HCL’s revenue share of the revenue reported by the partners for support services and new license sales in the current quarter. ARR excludes upfront revenue recognized on sale of perpetual licenses, professional services and any other non-recurring revenue.ARR is an operating metric, which should be viewed independently of revenue and is not a forecast of future revenues. Growth in ARR may not always be reflected in Revenue growth.
